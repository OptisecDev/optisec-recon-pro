Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

This policy describes what personal data the hosted OPTISEC Recon Pro service collects, why, where it is kept, and who else receives it. It describes what the software actually does today. If you run your own copy of OPTISEC Recon Pro (it is open source under the AGPL-3.0), you are the operator of that copy and this policy does not apply to it.

The hosted service is operated by OPTISEC (Ihsan Ali Atiyah Al-Qurayshi). TODO: confirm with Ehsan — operator / legal entity name and whether a postal address should be listed

1. What we collect

Account data

  • Username and email address you enter when you register.
  • Password — never stored in plain text. It is hashed with bcrypt (a unique random salt per password, default cost factor 12) and only the hash is stored.
  • API key — generated for your account; only a SHA-256 hash of it is stored.
  • Your role, your plan (Free / PRO / Enterprise), the date your account was created and the time of your last login.

Security and sign-in logs

  • For each sign-in, sign-out and registration attempt (successful or failed) we record the username entered, your IP address, the time, and the outcome. These records are used to rate-limit password guessing (5 failed attempts from one IP lock it out for 15 minutes) and are visible to administrators in the Admin Panel's auth log.
  • The rate-limiting counters themselves are held in server memory only.
  • Our hosting provider may also keep its own request logs (including IP addresses). TODO: confirm with Ehsan — Render request-log retention for this service

What you put into the platform

  • Targets (URLs/domains, names, notes), scans (which modules ran, results, and the authorization basis that allowed them), findings, and reports you generate.
  • Domain verification records: the domain, the verification method (DNS TXT or file), the token, and when it was last checked.
  • Dark-web / breach monitors: the domains or email addresses you ask us to watch, and the alerts found for them.
  • Records of CVE drafts, bug-bounty submissions and threat-intelligence shares you create (CVE drafts may include a reporter name and email if you enter them).
  • OSINT queries you run (for example a username, email, phone number or domain) are sent to the lookup services listed in section 4 to produce results.

Purchases

  • If you buy a PRO license, we store the email address you give at checkout, an order ID, the price and currency, the payment status, and the NOWPayments payment ID.
  • Payments are made in cryptocurrency on NOWPayments' hosted invoice page. We do not receive or store wallet credentials, card numbers or bank details.
  • License keys are stored only as hashes; the key itself is emailed to you once.

Cookies

  • access_token — your signed-in session (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production). It expires after a limited time and is refreshed while you are active.
  • csrf_secret — protects forms against cross-site request forgery; same lifetime as the session.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) is loaded only on the public landing page and the license purchase page, and only when the operator has configured it. When active, Google sets its own analytics cookies on those pages. No analytics run on the rest of the application. TODO: confirm with Ehsan — whether GA_MEASUREMENT_ID is set in production

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the scans, lookups and reports you request and show you the results.
  • To enforce scan authorization (domain verification) and keep an audit trail of why each scan was allowed.
  • To enforce plan limits and deliver and activate licenses you buy.
  • To detect and block abuse such as password guessing or unauthorized scanning.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

  • The application runs on Render. Its database is a hosted PostgreSQL database at Neon; all of the data in section 1 lives there. TODO: confirm with Ehsan — hosting regions for Render and Neon
  • Generated PDF reports and a local copy of the sign-in log are written to the server's disk, which on Render is temporary and is wiped on each restart or redeploy.
  • Connections to the database use TLS.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data, targets, scans, findings, reports, verification records and monitors are kept until the account (or the individual item) is deleted.
  • Sign-in log records and purchase records currently have no automatic expiry. TODO: confirm with Ehsan — retention period for auth_events and pending_payments
  • Unpaid checkout records are marked expired after 24 hours but are not deleted.

6. Deleting your data

There is no self-service "delete my account" button yet. To have your account deleted, email {{ contact_email }} from the address registered on the account. An administrator then deletes it, which permanently removes your account together with your targets, scans, findings, report records, domain verifications and dark-web monitors.

Not removed by that deletion: sign-in log entries (they are keyed by username and IP rather than linked to the account), purchase records (keyed by checkout email), and CVE drafts, bug-bounty submission records and threat-sharing records, which are kept but detached from your account. You can ask for these to be removed in the same email. TODO: confirm with Ehsan — response time for deletion requests and whether these residual records are purged on request

You can delete individual targets and domain verifications yourself at any time.

7. The demo account

The public demo account is shared by everyone who uses it. Anything you enter while signed in as demo may be seen by other visitors. Do not enter personal or sensitive information there.

8. Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, API keys and license keys are stored as hashes, session cookies are HttpOnly, and forms are CSRF-protected. No system is perfectly secure; to report a vulnerability, see SECURITY.md in the project repository or email the contact below.
